DEMO KNOWLEDGE DOCUMENT — Crop Health and Pest Monitoring
Source status: Illustrative starter content for testing retrieval only. Replace with reviewed ICAR / State Agricultural University / Kisan Call Centre material before operational use.

Integrated pest management begins with regular field observation and correct identification of the crop, growth stage, affected plant part and visible symptoms. A farmer can record the date, field location, crop variety, approximate area affected, number of affected plants and clear photographs.

Look at both healthy and affected plants. Observe whether symptoms are spreading, whether insects or eggs are visible, and whether damage is concentrated in field edges or particular patches. Similar symptoms can have different causes, including insects, disease, nutrient stress, water stress or weather conditions.

Do not select a pesticide only from a photograph or a general symptom description. Confirm the pest or disease and consult current recommendations from the relevant State Agricultural University, ICAR institution or local agriculture extension officer. Any pesticide use must follow the currently approved product label, crop registration, safety instructions, protective equipment requirements and local regulations.

Keep a record of field observations, diagnosis, advice received, action taken and follow-up results. If the cause is uncertain, seek help from a qualified agricultural extension worker rather than guessing.
